01 / PILOT

W/S flies; A/D steers. The chase camera turns with your heading. Q climbs; E descends. SHIFT boosts and breaks missile locks. The UFO has 3 recharging boost charges, each lasting at least 1.2 seconds; steering and tractor capture work during boosts.

02 / COLLECT

UFO: hold SPACE to tether a target below. Fly freely once it locks. White signal beacons: hold SPACE within 150m and below 500m AGL for 2.5 seconds to scan. Climb above aircraft to capture them and assimilate better flight systems and weapons.

03 / EVADE

SAM radar builds a lock, launches missiles, and scrambles interceptors. Automatic flares defend every 7 seconds. Hold F for the UFO attack laser. Press R to fire a homing missile at the marked hostile (4 rounds, one recharges every 9 seconds). Press T or tap the lock marker to cycle hostiles. The earned TR-3B locks lasers and missiles from orbit; altitude and tier increase its guided strike payload. Enemy pilots dodge and fight back during capture.

04 / ADVANCE

Complete the listed mission, then reach the blue extraction beacon. In Intercept mode, hold SPACE to attack UFOs; SR-71 pilots designate them for friendly SAM strikes. Capture an SR-71 intact to unlock the TR-3B triangular airframe, then press V to switch or choose it in this menu. Further aircraft captures level up its flight systems and weapons. Q/E reaches an orbital layer with free horizontal flight beyond the map; return over the terrain to descend. GO engages extraction autopilot when goals are complete. Follow the six operations to rescue Nem, Vek, Sera, Lio, Orr and Tal. The next operation saves in this browser after each success.

Touch: left pad moves, ALT + / − changes height, BEAM / FIRE is the main action, LASER attacks, MISSILE fires at the locked hostile, and BOOST spends a recharging charge. Shields recharge after 5 seconds without damage.
